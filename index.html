01 / BEHIND THE DECKS

THE ARTIST

Rusty Nuttz is an up-and-coming DJ and producer putting a fresh twist on hard trance. Known for his dynamic sets and genre-bending style, Rusty blends the energy of the harder sounds with his own unique flavour — pushing boundaries while keeping the rave spirit alive.

His productions don’t follow the usual formula. Instead, Rusty’s tracks carve their own path, weaving together influences from across the spectrum of dance music. It’s hard trance at the core, but reimagined with bold ideas, unexpected turns, and a focus on groove as much as grit.

As a DJ, Rusty delivers high-energy sets built for the dancefloor — no filler, just pure impact. Whether he's warming up the room or taking things into peak-time territory, his passion for the craft and connection to the crowd are always front and centre.

Rusty has already started making a name for himself in the underground scene, playing at prestigious events such as HTE Weekender 2024 & 2025, Hindsight/ NYE at Hindsight, High Oaks Festival, Reaction, Rebirth, bionic grand final and many more — all alongside some of the biggest names in the game.

Still early in his journey, Rusty Nuttz is just getting started, and the best is yet to come.
